1. Connect your camera

Aim a steady camera straight at the gym scoreboard. Connect it as a webcam or USB video capture device, then allow camera access. No microphone is needed.

2. Mark the numbers

Choose an area below, then drag a tight box around its numbers in the camera picture. Include the clock colon or decimal point. In the final minute, the reader uses whole seconds before the decimal (15.1 becomes 0:15). Keep team labels and fouls outside the boxes. To read the quarter, enable it and draw a separate box around its number.

Left/right means the teams on your overlay. Check the camera boxes match the correct physical scoreboard teams. Camera position and boxes save in this browser.

Reading adjustments

Boxy scoreboard digits reads the seven bars directly, including tightly spaced and slanted digits. Several camera frames are combined to reduce flicker. Use Ordinary printed numbers only for regular fonts. Keep the whole number inside each box and choose the matching bright/dark appearance.
