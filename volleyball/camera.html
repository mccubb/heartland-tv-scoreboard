Volleyball camera reader

Read the volleyball gym scoreboard and update your Heartland TV overlay.

Manual controls · Open scoreboard · Download reader code

1. Connect your camera

Aim a steady camera straight at the gym scoreboard. Connect it as a webcam or USB video capture device, then allow camera access. No microphone is needed.

2. Mark the numbers

Choose an area below, then drag a tight box around its numbers in the camera picture. Keep labels, periods, and fouls outside the boxes.

All four boxes are required: left score, right score, left sets, and right sets.

Left/right means the two team positions you assign from the camera view to the matching overlay teams. Check the camera boxes match the correct physical scoreboard teams. Camera position and boxes save in this browser.

Reading adjustments

If Test reading is wrong, tighten the boxes, zoom the camera closer, or change the digits appearance. This now uses the same basketball seven-segment number engine directly.

This camera reader sends the four live numbers to the streaming computer using the same cross-computer sync method as the matchup graphic. Keep this page open while broadcasting. Automatic updates confirm repeated readings before changing the overlay. All four fields—both scores and both sets—are read continuously. Unclear frames keep the last confirmed number instead of blanking or changing it. Clear boxes only removes the saved reading areas; it does not reset points or sets. Keep the camera picture visible while running. First use downloads the reading engine; camera frames are processed on this device.
